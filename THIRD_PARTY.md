# Third-party material

## Godot Engine editor icons

`src/renderer/src/icons/*.svg` are copied from the Godot Engine editor (`editor/icons/`, tag 4.4-stable,
https://github.com/godotengine/godot). Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
Copyright (c) Godot Engine contributors, with the Godot logo and name not used in this application. The only change is that
the icon colours are replaced with `currentColor` when the icons are loaded (`components/Icon.tsx`).

## Fonts

Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1), bundled through `@fontsource` packages.
